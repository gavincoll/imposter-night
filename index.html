How to play

  1. Pass the phone round. Each player holds the card to see the secret word, then lets go and passes it on.
  2. One of you is the imposter and doesn't get the word. With hints on, they see the category.
  3. Take turns saying one word that relates to the secret word. Go round twice.
  4. Spot the faker. Talk it over, then everyone points at who they suspect.
  5. Unmask. If the imposter is caught, they get one guess at the word to steal the win.
Example: the word is Pancake. Players say "flip", "Tuesday", "syrup". The imposter only knows the category is Food, hears "flip" and bluffs "batter". Nice save.

Best with 4 to 10 players. With 7 or more, try two imposters.
